Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do immediately if I get hacked? expand_more
Immediately disconnect from the internet to stop data transfer. Change your passwords from a different, secure device. Notify your bank and scan your system with reputable anti-malware software.
How often should I really change my password? expand_more
Experts now recommend focusing on password strength rather than frequent changes. However, if you suspect a breach or every 3-6 months is a good practice for critical accounts like banking and email.
Is public Wi-Fi safe to use with a VPN? expand_more
A VPN (Virtual Private Network) significantly improves safety on public Wi-Fi by encrypting your traffic. Without one, public Wi-Fi is generally unsafe for sensitive transactions.
How do I report a cyber crime effectively? expand_more
Contact your local law enforcement immediately. In the US, report to CISA or the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3). Keep logs of all suspicious activity.
